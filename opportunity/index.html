Undergraduate Research Opportunity

Wildfire Modeling

  1. Undergraduate research opportunity on wildfire modeling under the Montana NSF EPSCoR SMART FIRES seed fund.

    2. Position Overview
    I am seeking motivated undergraduate students with a foundational background in fluid mechanics and an interest in scientific computing to assist with research in CFD. This position is designed to provide hands-on research experience while building technical and analytical skills in a supportive academic environment.

    Required Qualifications
    • Undergraduate standing in engineering or a related field
    • Familiarity with at least one programming language (e.g., MATLAB, Python, or C++)
    • Strong interest in computational modeling and numerical simulation

    Preferred Qualifications
    • Prior exposure to CFD concepts through coursework or projects
    • Basic experience with Linux or willingness to learn
    • Ability to work independently and follow research protocols

    Key Responsibilities
    • Run and monitor computational fluid dynamics simulations under supervision
    • Assist in analyzing CFD results using scientific and engineering software
    • Organize, document, and summarize results in technical reports (Microsoft Word)
    • Prepare figures and presentation materials for group meetings and presentations (Microsoft PowerPoint)

    Learning Outcomes and Benefits
    • Research exposer
    • Exposure to open-source CFD models and research-grade simulation tools
    • Fundamental understanding of the physics of prescribed fires and wildfires
    • Development of data analysis and visualization skills using Python, MATLAB, and commercial software

    Salary
    As per Montana Tech Student Employee Job Classification and Wage Guidelines: https://www.mtech.edu/financial-aid/student-employment/index.html

    Interested students may send their CV to bmahato@mtech.edu
